Painted Bike Lane, Small

Painted lane right next to parked cars

Use caution, car doors can open suddenly

Consider using a different route

Painted Bike Lane, Large

Provides more space between rider and parked cars, or is along curb

Less risk of being hit with car door

Chill Side Street

Recommended side street with some combination of low traffic, stop signs, speed bumps

Intersection along these routes either have signals or 4-way stops

Most of these routes have speedbumps

Some of these routes are designated "greenways" or have sharrows

Protected Bike Lanes

Separated from car traffic by concrete or by plastic bollards.

Paved Trail

Off-street paved trail, access path to a trail, bike/pedestrian bridge, or very wide sidewalk

Unpaved Trail
